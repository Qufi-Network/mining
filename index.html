The model

Proof of Contribution

Proof of work rewards computing power. Proof of stake rewards money locked up. Proof of Contribution rewards what the QuFi network actually runs on: phones that stay online and do their signing work, each one proven by a key inside the phone.

The contribution is the work

Staying reachable and answering signing rounds is what keeps Palm vaults working. Nothing is burned to earn a ticket.

Every contribution is proven

Each heartbeat is signed by a key in the phone's security chip and carries the latest public random number, so it cannot be made in advance or copied to another device.

Spread wide, not bought

One ticket for being online, at most one more for doing the work, and a cap on phones per person. More money or more servers buy no extra chances.

How it works

Four steps, every 10 minutes

Mining runs on the phones that already keep the network running. Staying online is the work.

One round
  1. 01

    Stay online

    The QuFi Node app sends a signed heartbeat every 5 minutes. Each one carries the latest public random number, so it cannot be made in advance.

  2. 02

    Get one ticket

    Each round, every eligible phone gets exactly one ticket, worked out from the round's random number and the phone's own hardware key.

  3. 03

    The draw

    Tickets below the round's target win. The target adjusts like Bitcoin's difficulty, so the network mines the planned amount however many phones join.

  4. 04

    Win 1 $Q

    Anyone can check a win from public data alone. The $Q is credited to the phone owner's account.

Who can win

Four checks, every round

A phone is in the draw only when all four hold. Uptime is the easiest thing to fake, so each ticket has to cost one real phone, one licence and one person.

A node licence

The phone joined with an invite or licence signed by the network key.

A real phone

Its key lives in the phone's security chip, proven by Android Key Attestation. Emulators and servers cannot fake that proof.

Online for the last day

A heartbeat in every 5 minute slot for 24 hours, with up to 10 minutes of grace for a network blip.

One per person

Each person mines with a capped number of phones. Extra phones earn nothing more.

A fair draw

Nobody can rig the random number

Think of a raffle where every ticket was printed the day before, and the winning number is drawn by many independent referees who each sign it. Nobody can change a ticket, and nobody can pick the number.

How the draw works

Made by many, not one

The number comes from drand, run by the independent operators of the League of Entropy. None of them knows it in advance or can set it alone.

Signed and checked

Each number is signed. The app holds drand's public key and refuses any number whose signature does not check out.

Everything fixed first

Phone keys, who is eligible and the target are all settled before the number exists, so nothing can be adjusted after seeing it.

No backdoor

If drand ever stopped, mining would simply pause. It never switches to a number a person picks.

Two kinds of contribution

Doing the work earns a second ticket

Proof of Contribution counts two things. Uptime earns a phone its first ticket. A phone that answers 95% or more of the signing work it is asked for over 7 days gets one extra ticket each round. Never more than two.

Counts

  • Answering a signing round on time
  • Correctly refusing a bad request
  • Helping make a new vault's key
  • Later: vouching for fellow nodes' heartbeats

Never counts

  • Approving a payment. A node paid per signature would be paid to say yes, the opposite of what keeps vaults safe.
  • A refusal of a bad request scores exactly the same as a signature on a good one.

Supply

Built like Bitcoin

$Q follows Bitcoin's schedule. The $Q mined per round halves every 210,000 rounds, about every 4 years. The first era mines half of all $Q, each era after it half of the one before, approaching 50,000,000 and never passing it.
